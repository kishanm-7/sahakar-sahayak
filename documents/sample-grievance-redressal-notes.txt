RAISING A COMPLAINT ABOUT A COOPERATIVE SOCIETY — GENERAL PROCEDURE

Cooperative societies are registered and supervised under a cooperative act.
For most societies that is the act of the state they operate in. For societies
whose operations span more than one state, it is the central multi-state act.
Which act applies determines who hears a complaint, so this is the first thing
to establish.

STEP ONE: RAISE IT WITH THE SOCIETY FIRST

Put the complaint in writing to the secretary or the managing committee of the
society. Keep a copy. Ask for an acknowledgement with a date on it. Most
authorities will ask whether this step was taken before they will look at a
complaint themselves.

STEP TWO: THE REGISTRAR OF COOPERATIVE SOCIETIES

If the society does not respond, or the response is unsatisfactory, the
complaint goes to the Registrar of Cooperative Societies for the district or
the state. The Registrar's office handles matters such as disputes between a
member and the society, irregularities in elections, audit objections, and
misuse of funds.

WHAT A WRITTEN COMPLAINT SHOULD CONTAIN

The complainant's name, address and contact number.
The full name and registration number of the society.
The membership number, if the complainant is a member.
A clear statement of what happened, with dates.
What has already been tried, and what response was received.
What outcome is being asked for.
Copies of supporting papers such as receipts, passbook entries, loan documents
or letters.

Vague complaints are the slowest to move. Dates, amounts and document copies
make a complaint much harder to set aside.

OTHER ROUTES

Complaints about a scheme rather than about a society can also be filed on the
centralised public grievance portal run by the Government of India.
Complaints about the banking conduct of a cooperative bank may fall under the
Reserve Bank of India's ombudsman scheme for that class of bank.

KEEP THE REFERENCE NUMBER

Whichever route is used, a reference or acknowledgement number is issued. That
number is the only reliable way to follow up later, so it should be written
down and kept safely.
